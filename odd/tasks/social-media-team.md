# social-media-team

## Objective

A replicable social media "agency" inside team-harness: optional agents that take a brand from strategy to published and measured content. Piloted on nuriaromero.dev, then installable in any client project.

## Problem and why

The owner publishes rarely and by hand. She wants constant, mostly automated content (carousels and faceless video now, face video later) and wants to sell the same service to clients.

## Scope

- In: strategy, competitor research, editorial calendar, copy in the brand voice, carousels, faceless video (reuse `~/Desktop/code/nuriaromerodev/proyectos internos nuriaromero.dev/reels-pipeline`, repo nuriaromerodev/reels-pipeline, moved there on 2026-11-26), scheduling and analytics. Since 2026-11-25 scheduling goes through self-hosted Postiz for Instagram, Facebook and YouTube and through the Metricool connector for TikTok only (feature `odd/tasks/social-publishing-postiz.md`).
- Later: LinkedIn (through Postiz, waiting for the LinkedIn app approval), face-video editing. Facebook is connected through Postiz since 2026-11-23.
- Out: paid ads, influencer management.

## Constraints

- Free tools only unless the owner approves a cost. Metricool Free limits are unverified.
- Never hide watermarks; no avatar video with the owner's face without her upload.
- Nothing is published without the owner's approval until she says otherwise.
- The module must be optional at harness adoption and addable later.
- TDD: strict mode enabled (source: user global config). Runner for harness code: the team-harness repo's own test command. Not applicable to the strategy documents.
