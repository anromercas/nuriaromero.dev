# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners, managers, and decision-makers of local businesses in Seville where
each new client matters. They need a clear way to assess whether local SEO
can improve their visibility and lead generation without technical jargon or
unfounded guarantees.

## Product Purpose

Nuria Romero's website presents direct web, software, automation, AI, and
local SEO services. Its service pages help qualified prospects understand the
offer and reserve an introductory call.

## Positioning

The local SEO service combines hands-on Google Business Profile work with
technical web implementation. Clients communicate directly with the person
who audits, configures, and improves the work.

## Operating Context

The SEO local service page targets Seville businesses. Its primary conversion
is reserving a call. The site is an Astro web application with reusable
service-page components and a booking CTA.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Plan Local: 300 EUR/month, taxes not included.
- Plan Local Pro: 500 EUR/month, taxes not included; the recommended plan.
- There is no standalone initial project and no confirmed contractual minimum
  term.
- The page must not state or imply guarantees of rankings, traffic, leads, or
  recommendations by AI assistants.
- General-audience copy must avoid clinic-specific language such as patients
  or treatments.
- The public message must remain close, professional, and free of unexplained
  jargon.

## Brand Commitments

The service is presented as direct, transparent, technically competent, and
honest about what SEO can and cannot guarantee.

## Evidence on Hand

- Current commercial brief: `odd/tasks/seo-local-sevilla.md`.
- Existing SEO local service route: `src/pages/seo-local-sevilla.astro`.
- User-provided plan reference from a clinic proposal; its deliverables are
  applicable, but its clinic-specific wording is not.
- No verified public performance metrics, rankings, testimonials, or case
  study outcomes may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Explain value in business language before technical mechanism.
2. Make the commercial scope and next action easy to understand.
3. Preserve factual accuracy over persuasive exaggeration.
4. Design for qualified conversations, not vanity metrics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The web experience must maintain semantic structure, keyboard access,
readable contrast, and responsive layouts.
