## 1. Summary

- Starting point (NRD-21 baseline, 2026-11-14): 36 backlinks, 35 referring domains, 33 main referring domains, `backlinks_spam_score` 59; 27 of the 36 backlinks carry a PBN-sale anchor that nobody here asked for. There are no brand or service anchors written by real third parties.
- Since then (latest recorded, 2026-11-21): 38 referring domains, 8 of them nofollow, 35 main domains. The growth is +3 referring domains in 7 days (35 on 2026-11-15, 38 on 2026-11-21). Nothing in the records says which domains were gained, so the growth cannot be attributed to any real mention (see section 2).
- AI mentions: the Google AI-mentions tool found no mentions for `nuriaromero.dev` on 2026-11-21 (the daily reports of 2026-11-15 to 2026-11-20 say the same). For `citysem.es` the same tool returns 172 on 2026-11-21.
- On the head term `agencia seo sevilla` the AI Overview cites agencies plus two third-party lists (`ip10.es`, `pacoruben.com`), and page 1 holds at least six list or directory pages. Being listed in those lists is the shortest recorded path to a mention (SEO report 2026-11-21).
- Section 4 lists 22 existence-checked targets, ranked by fit then effort. 5 come from NRD-72 (ranks 1, 2, 3, 21, 22), 3 from the NRD-21 competitor study (ranks 5, 16, 17), 3 from the SERP of `agencia seo sevilla` recorded on 2026-11-21 (ranks 6, 7, 8), and 11 from the developer's public reads (ranks 4, 9 to 15, 18 to 20: community, chamber, local media, associations). Sortlist and Clutch profiles already exist (`docs/seo-audit/local-signals-2026-11.md`, 2026-11-18) and are not repeated as new targets.
- What is not available: backlink total and spam score after 2026-11-14; brand mentions on the open web; the optional story data step (DataForSEO) was not run, so there is no per-domain list of the 3 new referring domains. Details in section 2.
