# Keyword clusters per service, November 2026 (NRD-73)

SEO method phase 2 (epic NRD-4). Real Seville queries per in-focus service, grouped by SERP intent so that one cluster maps to one page. Input for phase 3 (NRD-74). This document records observations and proposals only; no page, copy, URL, link, `src/data/seo-intents.ts` or `keywordOwners` entry was changed. Analysis is in English; queries are in Spanish, verbatim.

In scope: diseño web, tienda online, SEO local, software a medida, IA / automatizaciones, GEO. Out of scope: parked niche pages and `/seo-para-clinicas-capilares-sevilla/`.

## How to read this document

- **Dato observado**: a figure or fact taken from a named source with its data date. **Inferencia**: a reading of those facts. The two are never mixed in one cell; where a section needs both, they are labelled.
- **gap** means "not measured". It is never 0 and never an estimate. No volume, difficulty or ranking in this document is invented or estimated.
- **sin dato (<reason>)** means a DataForSEO call was made for the query (S10, 2026-11-15) and returned no value; the reason is the tool's own result (absent from the database, row with no volume fields, no difficulty value). It is not 0 either. After NRD-97, the 22 queries that read "gap" for volume read either a measured figure with source and date or "sin dato (<reason>)".
- Volumes are Spain-level (Labs and Keyword Planner are country-level); SERPs are read at Seville (city level). A Labs volume is therefore not a Seville volume.

S5 records `localPack.present: false` for the same keyword (mobile capture, 2026-11-13). The two inputs disagree on a point of fact; the cause (device, capture depth or parser) is not known. The cluster tables use S1/S2 for features and S5 only for the top 3 domains, and flag the disagreement.
- **Volume of `seo local sevilla`.** Labs overview says 50 per month (range 10 to 90, S1); the Labs domain intersection quotes 70 (S1, same call date); Keyword Planner shows the 500 bucket (S6, exports of 2026-09-20 and 2026-10-08) and the 50 bucket (S6, export of 2026-10-09). Three figures, three methods, none estimated or averaged here.
- **Volume of `agencia seo sevilla`.** Labs overview 1,000 (S1); Keyword Planner bucket 5000 (S6, export of 2026-09-20) and bucket 500 (S6, export of 2026-10-09).
- **Volume of `posicionamiento seo sevilla`.** Keyword Planner bucket 500 in the export of 2026-09-20 and bucket 50 in the export of 2026-10-09 (S6); Labs domain intersection 110 (S1).
- **Local-pack entry "Diseño Web Sevilla".** S10 call 8 (`diseño tienda online sevilla`, 2026-11-15) lists it with 1 review; S3 (`diseño web sevilla`, 2026-11-15) lists the same name with 13 reviews, and S10 call 19 lists "Diseño Web Sevilla" with 13. Same name, different review counts: not known whether it is one listing, and not resolved. The TO-1 -> DW-1 merge counts only the member that does not depend on this (Gráfica Estudio Creativo).
