# Surface brief: /seo-local-sevilla/

Scope: Persuade. Whole-page redesign inside the established nuriaromero.dev world (dark, Onest). Audience: Seville local-business owners. Action: reserve a call. Proof allowed: Google reviews (live), Arkady and ADF Sevilla projects, "+10 anos", Google rating/count. No invented results.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page shows the mechanism instead of describing it: a drawn Google business listing that fills in piece by piece as the visitor scrolls. Refuses the text-on-dark-panels audit sheet and the equal icon-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Ink-blue near-black ground (the site's gray-950 family), sky-blue as the "signal" colour for map and listing drawings, the site's yellow reserved for the recommended plan and primary action emphasis. Onest for everything; Instrument Serif italic only for one accent phrase per heading. Hand-built SVG illustrations in one stroke weight, hairline rules, rounded listing cards. Recognizable with copy removed by the drawn listing, the asymmetric bento of mini-drawings, and the circular monthly cycle.

STORY: A Seville owner understands in seconds that someone will fix how their business appears on Google, sees exactly which pieces get reviewed, believes it because the person is real (photo, reviews, two real client sites), and books a call. Honest about limits: no promised rankings.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column: H1 with the accent phrase in serif italic, a two-line subtitle, "Reservar una llamada" primary button and a quiet text link to the plans. Right column: the drawn listing card (business name placeholder, category, hours, photos strip, rating row, services chips) with small labelled ticks appearing, tagged "Ejemplo ilustrativo". Under the fold line of the viewport a thin data row: +10 anos de experiencia, Google rating and review count (hidden when unavailable), 2 webs de negocios locales en Sevilla.

FORM: Persuade landing, user-pinned structure (no concept roll): hero + proof row, asymmetric bento of what is reviewed, how-we-work with real photo and monthly cycle diagram, real proof (reviews + two projects), plans, FAQ, closing CTA.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
